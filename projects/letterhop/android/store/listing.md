## en-GB

**App name** (max 30): `LetterHop: Word Ladder`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Go from one word to another, one letter at a time. Fewer words, more points!`

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Turn one word into another by changing one letter at a time. Every step must be a real word: can you reach the end with as few words as possible?

THREE LEVELS
• Easy: at least 4 words between start and end
• Medium: at least 6 words
• Hard: at least 8 words
Choose words of 5 or 6 letters.

FEWER WORDS, MORE POINTS
• Use as many words as you like: there is always a way to the end
• Every word over the minimum costs points
• Earn up to three stars for each puzzle

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
• Tips show which letter to change, or the whole next word
• Undo is free: only your final chain counts
• After each puzzle, compare your chain with a shortest route

FIVE LANGUAGES
• Real dictionaries in English, Italian, Spanish, French and German
• Thousands of puzzles, each one checked so that the minimum is exact

PRIVACY-FIRST & OFFLINE
• Works offline after the first launch
• Light and dark theme
• No sign-up, no personal data collection
• 100% free with no ads
```
