## en-GB

**App name** (max 30): `Sea Battle: Naval Strategy`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Hide your fleet and sink theirs: play friends online, on one phone or the AI.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
The classic naval battle game: deploy your fleet on the grid, take turns firing at the enemy waters and be the first to sink every ship!

EVERY WAY TO PLAY
• Online with friends: create a game and share the 6-character code or the invite link
• Random opponent: get paired with someone who speaks your language
• Same device: two captains pass the phone, and a screen hides each fleet from the other
• Against the computer: three levels, from relaxed to ruthless, with a hint when you need one

FAIR AND CLEAR
• Five ships on a 10 × 10 grid; ships never touch, not even at the corners
• Optional rule: a hit gives you another shot
• Online, fleets stay on the server: nobody can peek or cheat
• Clock, chat and rematch; if your opponent disconnects for 2 minutes, you win automatically

PRIVACY-FIRST
• No registration or login: open the app and play
• 100% free, no ads, no tracking
• Online rooms are deleted automatically within 48 hours of the last activity
• Plays offline too, on one device or against the computer
• English, Italian, Spanish, French and German; light and dark theme
```

### Data safety (Play Console → App content → Data safety)

- Data collected: the nickname the player types and the in-game chat messages, for online games only. Declare **Personal info → Name** and **Messages → Other in-app messages**, purpose **App functionality**, not shared with third parties. Nothing else is collected: no email, phone, location or device identifiers.
- Games on one device or against the computer send nothing to the server.
- Temporary data: rooms, nicknames, fleets and chat are deleted automatically within 48 hours of the last activity.
- Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No ad networks, tracking SDKs or profiling.
- Content rating questionnaire: no violence beyond abstract ships on a grid, user-to-user chat **yes** (text only, between two players of a game).
