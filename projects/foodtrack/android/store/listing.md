## en-GB

**App name** (max 30): `FoodTrack: Food & Expiry`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Track food at home, get expiry alerts, share shopping lists and find recipes.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
FoodTrack helps you keep the food at home under control, waste less and always know what to cook.

KNOW WHAT YOU HAVE
• Organise your fridge, freezer, pantry and any other space you like
• Add products from a catalogue of over 150 foods, or scan the barcode
• Quantities, expiry dates, brand and Nutri-Score in one place

NEVER MISS AN EXPIRY DATE
• Expired and expiring products are clearly highlighted
• Choose how many days in advance you want to be reminded
• Practical tips to tell whether a product is still good to eat

SHOPPING LISTS THAT KEEP UP
• Create as many lists as you need (weekly shop, party, holiday…)
• Put items in the cart while you shop
• Once home, move them into a space with one tap to track their expiry

COOK WITH WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
• Over 100 simple recipes from Italy, the UK, Ireland, the US, France, Spain, Greece, Japan, India and many more countries
• Short, step-by-step instructions
• See at a glance which ingredients you have and which are missing
• Recipes that use your expiring products come first

PLAN THE WEEK
• Weekly meal plan for breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner
• Add the missing ingredients to your shopping list automatically

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
• Invite family or flatmates with a code
• Everyone sees and updates the same spaces, lists and meal plan, always in sync
• Create several households (home, holiday home, office…)

AND ALSO
• Sign in with email or with your Google account
• Light and dark theme
• Available in English and Italian
• Free, no ads, no tracking: your data is never sold or used for advertising

Expiry tips are indicative and do not replace your own judgement.
```
