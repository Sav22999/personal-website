# Course logos

The white logos in the course badges. Every file here is a single-colour SVG drawn as a mask over the course colour
(`.fam .logo` in `assets/fucina.css`, chosen by `LOGOS` / `COURSE_LOGOS` in `assets/app.js`).

- `savcomponents.svg`: the SavComponents logo, by Saverio Morelli (own work).
- Every other file: [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.36.0, whose SVG data is released under
  [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). Only the `<title>` was removed.

endorsed by them. Logos left out on purpose, so their courses keep the text badge: those under a non-commercial licence

SQL Server, Excel, Power BI, Fluent, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Grok/xAI, Lumo).
